The route crossing

Zone A is the route across the grounding line (km 24–60 from Depot 43D), B the blue-ice ponds on the way to Maitri (km 60–100), C the grounding zone around the crossing, where melt usually shows first.

Stations: Maitri and Novolazarevskaya

Both stations stand on rock in the Schirmacher Oasis and run warmer than the ice; they have about 60 % more days above 0 °C than the shelf. Degree days are a better guide to melt than counts of warm days.

What past seasons show
  • Melt starts at the grounding zone and in the blue-ice belt, then spreads along the channels to the terminal lake; the ice front comes last (Dell et al. 2024; project analysis).
  • Zone A, 2002–25: first wet day median 11 Dec, earliest 8 Nov (2007), latest 22 Dec. Recent: 21 Nov 2017, 5 Dec 2019, 10 Dec 2024, 27 Nov 2025.
  • Shelf-wide: half the shelf wet from about 17 Dec to 25 Jan; peak about 4 Jan.
  • Channel water builds from about 12–20 Dec; in lake years water reaches the terminal lake 27 Dec–11 Jan.
  • Snowfall delays ponding; positive degree days follow melt closely (the five warmest seasons were the five biggest melt seasons).
How this page works
  • Passive microwave: AMSR2 wet-snow product (Picard & Fily, Grenoble), 10 km, daily, about 10 days behind. A cell counts as wet when either daily pass is wet.
  • Sentinel-1: radar every few days through cloud; change from the October median of the same orbit. Wet snow is darker; 3 dB or more is the usual mark.
  • Sentinel-2 / Landsat: clear days only; open water where the water index is high and the pixel is at least twice as dark as in October (keeps blue ice out). Below about 2 km² in zone A, low sun can give false water.
  • NISAR: L-band radar every 12 days, also senses water below the surface; provisional data.
  • Stations: 6-hourly reports via Ogimet and NOAA GHCN daily values. Forecast: AMPS, from the MINIS weather site.
  • Checked once a day, plus station reports every 6 hours, until the end of February 2027. Code and rules: github.com/vikramonice/nivl-melt-watch.
